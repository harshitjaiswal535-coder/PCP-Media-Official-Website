THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Good questions. Clear answers.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope, assets and feedback cycles. After the initial brief, we'll suggest a realistic timeline before work begins.

What deliverables will I receive?

Deliverables are agreed in the project scope and may include a strategy document, content calendar, creative assets, edited video, campaign plan or performance summary.

How will we collaborate?

We align on a brief, milestones and feedback process at the start. You’ll know what is being created, when reviews happen and what is needed from you.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the services, volume of content, production needs, platforms and timeline. We’ll discuss your goals and budget before sharing a tailored proposal.

How do we get started?

Share your business, goals, preferred services and timing through the contact page or WhatsApp. We’ll review the brief and discuss the best next step.
